Z-Anatomy human anatomy atlas, adapted for ASTRA-MED

Source: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
This app distributes a compressed GLB export of selected Z-Anatomy system meshes. Upstream non-commercial reference parts (including kidneys and inner-ear/cochlear structures) are excluded from the export.
